Why Build Here

Natural Rock.
Tropical Slope.

Building on Mahé's granite hillside is not a construction challenge — it is a construction advantage. The rock provides a permanent, immovable foundation that no engineered base can replicate.

Each villa plot is terraced into the natural contours of the slope, ensuring maximum privacy from neighbours and unobstructed views across the Indian Ocean.

Class 1 Construction License — highest classification in Seychelles
In-house design and engineering team — full project control
No subcontractors — all works performed by Kensington's own teams
