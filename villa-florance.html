The residence

Classical proportions, a private courtyard

A two-storey classical villa arranged around a central courtyard, with six bedrooms in the proposed layout. Architectural concrete, decorative mouldings and metal balcony railings give the façades their character. The layout may be adapted to the selected plot and the client’s requirements. Plot suitability, views and final specifications are confirmed individually.

Plans and elevations

Explore the proposed layout

The ground floor shows the main living and dining area, four bedrooms and spaces for a gym and spa. The upper floor has two bedrooms, a lounge and balconies. The plan can be revised for the selected plot and client brief. Wellness equipment and the pool are quoted separately.

Base specification

What the price includes

Included in the base price

The base price includes a levelled building plot, villa construction, a finished façade and roof, windows and entrance doors, connections to central estate utilities, internal plumbing, water, drainage and electrical distribution, and walls, floors and ceilings prepared for final finishes.

Options quoted separately

Final interior finishes, equipment and outdoor upgrades are quoted separately for each client. Choose tile, marble, granite or timber flooring; paint, decorative plaster, wallpaper or stone for walls; lighting, chandeliers and accent lighting; kitchen equipment, furnishings, smart home systems and air conditioning; and a pool, landscape design, trees, planting and automatic irrigation. The quote reflects the brands, materials and quantities you select.
