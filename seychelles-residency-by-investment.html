Seychelles Residency Through Property Investment

Empathia Village · Owner's Guide · June 2026 · 6 min read

Across Europe, the Middle East and Asia, residency-by-investment programmes have become one of the most sought-after instruments of personal planning — and one by one, the classic options have been closing or raising their thresholds. Seychelles offers something quietly different: purchasing a home here may open the right to live here. No separate government fund contribution, no points system — residence options may accompany the property purchase for eligible buyers.

Who qualifies?

Purchasers of a villa at Empathia Village may be eligible for a Seychelles residence permit, subject to applicable regulations and individual circumstances confirmed during the transaction process. Where applicable, the permit covers the owner and their immediate family — spouse and children. The developer's legal team assists with the application process on your behalf.

What does the permit allow?

  • Year-round living. Reside in Seychelles as long as you wish — or simply keep the option open and visit when you choose.
  • Family included. Your spouse and children hold the same right to reside.
  • Education. Children of residents are eligible to attend local international schools on Mahé.
  • A stable second base. Seychelles is politically stable, English- and French-speaking, in a convenient time zone (GMT+4) between Europe and Asia, and outside the cyclone belt.
Seychelles is widely regarded as an attractive jurisdiction for international families. Tax treatment always depends on your personal circumstances and home-country rules — we recommend confirming details with your own advisors, and our team can introduce you to experienced local counsel.

How the process works

There is no separate "programme" to apply to — residency follows the property. The sequence is simple: reserve your villa and plot, complete the staged purchase as construction progresses, and on completion the freehold title is registered in your name and the residence permit application is processed. The whole journey is described in our step-by-step buyer's guide.

Residency that pays for itself

Unlike contribution-based programmes, where the qualifying payment is spent, here your capital is invested in a tangible, income-capable asset. A villa at Empathia Village holds full freehold title — transferable and inheritable — and may be rented out through professional villa management when you are away, benefiting from the strong year-round occupancy of the premium Seychelles market. Villas start from $800,000, with payments accepted in USD, EUR and GBP.
