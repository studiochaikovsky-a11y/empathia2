Frequently Asked Questions

Ownership & Legal
Can foreign nationals purchase property at Empathia Village?
+
Yes. Foreign buyers may acquire freehold title to villa and land in Seychelles, subject to applicable approvals and transaction procedures. The purchase process is fully supported by our legal team.
What type of title will I receive?
+
The proposed ownership is freehold for the villa and plot, subject to Government Sanction, independent title checks, the final contract and the applicable transaction process. Buyers should appoint a Seychelles lawyer to verify the selected property.
Do I qualify for Seychelles residency?
+
Residence options may be available to eligible purchasers under Seychelles regulations, confirmed individually during the transaction process. Where applicable, the permit covers the owner and their immediate family. Our legal team will advise on your specific eligibility during the purchase process. Children of qualifying residents may be eligible to attend local international schools.
Payment & Finance
What currencies are payments accepted in?
+
Payments are accepted in USD, EUR and GBP. International transfers are welcome and can be made from Seychelles or abroad. We work with clients across Europe, the Middle East and Asia and accommodate preferred banking jurisdictions. Please contact us for full payment documentation.
Is a staged payment plan available?
+
Yes. The proposed structure starts with a 20% initial payment. Later payments follow agreed construction stages after inspection and acceptance in accordance with the contract. Buyers may appoint an independent technical supervisor. The signed agreement defines the final milestones, evidence and remedies.
What does the price include?
+
The base scope includes the plot, villa structure, turnkey interior finishing, fencing, fitted kitchen, parking and central estate utility connections. A pool, premium materials, professional landscaping and furnishing are optional extras. The signed contract and technical schedules define the final scope.
Residences & Customisation
What are the minimum plot sizes?
+
All plots at Empathia Village are a minimum of 600 m². Villa Jane and Villa Anna start from 600 m² plots. Villa Georgette is positioned on plots of from 1,500 m². Larger plots and combined plots can be arranged on request.
Can the villa design be customised?
+
Yes. Every residence at Empathia Village can be individually adapted. We offer the ability to increase the house area, expand the plot, adapt the floor plan and refine the design in accordance with your personal vision. Our design and construction team will work with you from initial concept through to completion.
How long does construction take?
+
Each villa takes approximately 12 months to construct from groundbreaking. The timeline may vary depending on individual customisation requirements. Construction is carried out by Kensington Construction & Development, the estate developer holding a Class I construction licence in Seychelles.
Island Lifestyle
Can I rent my villa when I'm not using it?
+
Rental use depends on the intended model. Long-term residential letting and short-term tourist accommodation may have different licensing, management, estate and tax requirements. Buyers should confirm the permitted use with qualified Seychelles advisers. Rental returns and occupancy are not guaranteed.
Is an inspection visit possible before purchase?
+
Absolutely. We offer a private one-week discovery tour to the Seychelles with accommodation on Eden Island. Our team arranges all logistics, site visits and meetings with the construction team. Contact us to plan your visit.
What concierge services are available?
+
We assist our owners with the complete logistics of island living — from yacht and catamaran charter to vehicle import and customs clearance, domestic staffing, property management and island experiences. Our concierge team is available throughout the year.
