Construction Update

Phase I Construction Update —
Work Underway · Plots by Request

Empathia Village · Construction · May 2026 · 5 min read

Construction is underway at Empathia Village, with the first phase of private residences set on the granite terraces above Baie Lazare on the south-west coast of Mahé. The estate comprises 30 plots; current availability is shown on the masterplan, and adjacent available plots can be combined.

What Phase I progress means

Phase I establishes the construction sequence for the estate and allows prospective buyers to review visible site progress as part of their due diligence.

Phase I buyers are progressing through the agreed construction schedule. Foundation works for the first residences are underway. Construction is managed by Kensington Construction & Development, the Class I licensed developer with over 30 years of international experience building in complex tropical terrains.

What is available in Phase I

Phase I plots are set on the terraced hillside above Baie Lazare, each with unobstructed Indian Ocean views. Three villa designs are offered:

  • Villa Jane — from $990,000. 2 bedrooms, an optional private pool at additional cost, from 140 m² of living space on plots from 600 m². A considered, architecturally refined residence designed for comfortable year-round living or as a managed rental asset.
  • Villa Anna — from $1,500,000. A private pool is available at additional cost; from 240 m² with expanded terracing on plots from 600 m². Ideal for buyers who want additional space and a slightly elevated position on the hillside.
  • Villa Georgette — from $2,100,000. The estate's signature residence: 4 bedrooms, an optional panoramic infinity pool at additional cost, from 350 m² of living space with terraces up to 150 m², on plots from 1,500 m². The most private and elevated position on the estate, with views extending across the bay to the horizon.
All three villa designs are presented with freehold ownership of villa and plot, subject to Government Sanction, independent title checks and the final contract. Residence options, where available, are considered separately by the Seychelles authorities. Staged payments follow agreed construction milestones.

Construction timeline and progress

Site preparation at Empathia Village is complete. The granite hillside terrain of Mahé has been shaped and terraced to preserve the natural landscape while creating elevated plots. Road and foundation works are progressing in phases. Completion of the full estate is planned for 2030; plot-specific handover schedules are confirmed in the buyer documentation.

Progress photography and construction updates are shared with confirmed buyers throughout the build. Our team can arrange a private site visit to review the estate and available plots.

Why early phases matter

Early-stage buyers can compare a broader selection of plots, aspects and elevations. Any future change in value depends on market conditions and is not guaranteed.

There is also a practical dimension: plot selection. Phase II buyers choose among the plots currently offered for sale. Plots vary in aspect, elevation and size — and the best positions are claimed first. Once Phase II closes, as Phase I did, there will be no further opportunity to purchase at this stage of the project.

How to enquire

Our team responds to every enquiry within 24 hours with full villa specifications, plot maps, payment documentation and, where desired, arrangements for a one-week discovery tour of the estate with accommodation on Eden Island. To learn more about the three villa designs or to request a plot map for Phase II, contact us directly.
