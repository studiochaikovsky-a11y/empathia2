01 · Ownership

Freehold ownership is possible, subject to Government Sanction

Under Seychelles rules, a non-Seychellois may acquire privately owned immovable property after obtaining Government Sanction under the Immovable Property (Transfer Restriction) Act. Approval is not automatic and the precise procedure, timing and conditions depend on the buyer and the legal classification of the property.

The Seychelles Planning Authority states that a sanction is generally valid for one year. Buyers should appoint an independent Seychelles lawyer to confirm title, encumbrances, planning position, project classification and the conditions attached to the approval before funds are committed.

Empathia supports the document process, but the buyer and their lawyer remain responsible for independent legal and tax review.

02 · Buyer documents

Typical KYC and source-of-funds documents

The official guidance lists documents commonly required for a non-Seychellois acquisition:

  • Certified passport copy and proof of residential address.
  • Police clearance certificate and politically exposed person declaration.
  • Source-of-funds declaration and supporting proof of funds.
  • Additional corporate, beneficial-owner or family documents where applicable.

The authority or the buyer's advisers may request further documents for a particular application.

04 · Payment protection

Payments follow agreed construction stages

The proposed structure begins with a 20% initial payment. Later payments are linked to agreed construction stages and are due after inspection and acceptance of the relevant stage in accordance with the contract.

  • The buyer may appoint an independent lawyer and technical supervisor.
  • The contract should define each milestone, evidence of completion, inspection rights, notice periods and remedies.
  • There is currently no separate insurance-backed completion guarantee or performance bond represented on this website.

Never rely on a website summary alone. Ask your advisers to review the final payment schedule and protections before signing.

06 · Rental use

Licensing depends on how the villa is let

There is no blanket promise of unrestricted rental use. Long-term residential letting and short-term tourist accommodation are treated differently. A self-catering or other tourism operation may require a Seychelles Tourism Department licence, suitable management arrangements and tax compliance.

Before purchase, confirm the intended rental model, any estate rules, licence eligibility, permitted use, management fees and tax treatment with qualified Seychelles advisers. Occupancy and returns are not guaranteed.
