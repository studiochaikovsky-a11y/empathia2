Buyer guide · Updated September 2026

Know what you are buying

A concise due-diligence guide to ownership, scope, payments, costs and independent checks. It is designed to help you ask the right questions before reservation or contract.

OwnershipBuyer documentsIncluded scopePaymentsTransaction costsRental useDeveloperDue-diligence pack

01 · Ownership

Freehold with a clear Government Sanction procedure

For a qualifying non-Seychellois purchaser, freehold ownership of privately owned property is available subject to Government Sanction and the applicable legal process. Our team prepares the project documents and supports the buyer's application before the transaction proceeds to the relevant subsequent contractual stages. The authority makes the formal decision.

Available title, planning and transaction documents for the selected plot can be reviewed with the buyer's own Seychelles lawyer before proceeding. This includes project-specific documentation the team says is applicable to plots offered from 600 m²; its precise legal effect must be checked for the individual transaction.

We personally discuss the purchase structure, approval process and applicable documents with each buyer before payment obligations are agreed.

02 · Buyer documents

Typical KYC and source-of-funds documents

The official guidance lists documents commonly required for a non-Seychellois acquisition:

  • Certified passport copy and proof of residential address.
  • Police clearance certificate and politically exposed person declaration.
  • Source-of-funds declaration and supporting proof of funds.
  • Additional corporate, beneficial-owner or family documents where applicable.

The authority or the buyer's advisers may request further documents for a particular application.

03 · Commercial scope

Base scope and optional upgrades are separated

Base scopeOptional at additional cost
Private plot; villa structure; turnkey interior finishing; fencing; fitted kitchen; parking; connections to central estate utilities.Private pool and terrace; premium materials; professional landscaping; furnishing and décor packages.

Specifications, brands, quantities, exclusions, areas and completion standards must be confirmed in the signed contract and its technical schedules. Website images are illustrative.

04 · Contractual payment stages

Documents first, agreed schedule next

Before the relevant contractual payment stages, the purchaser receives the available documentation for the selected property, transaction structure and Government Sanction procedure. Payment amounts, dates, construction milestones and handover terms are defined in the individual signed contract.

  • The buyer may appoint an independent lawyer and technical supervisor.
  • The contract and technical specification identify the selected plot, villa, included scope, optional works and payment schedule.
  • Construction and title registration proceed according to the agreed documents and applicable approvals.

05 · Taxes and government fees

Transaction costs are explained separately

Empathia Village provides assistance with the Government Sanction process as part of transaction support. Government taxes, duties, registration charges and other applicable statutory transaction costs are payable separately by the purchaser and are not included in the advertised villa price. Before proceeding, the buyer receives information on the charges relevant to the selected property and transaction structure. We do not present one universal percentage because legal classification and individual circumstances matter. If approval cannot be obtained despite the buyer meeting the agreed conditions, the amount paid to reserve the plot is refunded under the reservation agreement.

Independent Seychelles legal and tax advisers can confirm the current rules and written estimates. See the official acquisition guidance and tax information.

06 · Owner's choice

Rental at the owner’s choice

Owners may choose to let their villa; this is not prohibited within Empathia Village.

The agency and the owner's advisers should confirm the intended use, any applicable tourist accommodation licence, estate requirements and tax treatment. No occupancy, rental rate or return is promised by the developer.

07 · Residence support

Personal consultation before purchase

We discuss available Seychelles residence procedures with prospective buyers and help prepare relevant documents. The team reports that past project clients have received permits. A purchase does not itself grant a permit: eligibility is individual and the final decision belongs to the competent Seychelles authority. Buyers may consult an independent lawyer before entering the transaction.

Licence and experience
