About
Empathia Village

Empathia Village is a gated community of 35 private luxury residences set on the hillside above Baie Lazare — one of Mahé Island's most celebrated bays — with the Indian Ocean as a constant backdrop.

The estate is positioned between two of the Indian Ocean's finest resorts: Four Seasons Resort Seychelles and Kempinski Seychelles Resort.

Elevated between 50 and 100 metres above sea level, the terraced layout ensures that most residences command uninterrupted panoramas of the ocean and the tropical sunset.

The Name

"Empathy is what
restores a lost paradise"

The name Empathia Village reflects the founding philosophy of the project: that a true home is not simply a building — it is a place that resonates with the people who live in it.

Each villa is designed and built with this in mind: generous proportions, natural materials, views that connect the interior to the landscape, and a pace of life that restores.
