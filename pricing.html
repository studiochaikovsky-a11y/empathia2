Residency Permit

Every villa purchase at Empathia Village grants an automatic Seychelles Residence Permit under the current foreign investment legislation.

Residence permits are granted to the primary buyer and family members. Seychelles is a stable, tax-efficient jurisdiction with no income tax, no capital gains tax and no inheritance tax.
