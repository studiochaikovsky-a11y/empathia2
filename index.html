FAQ

Before you request details

Can non-Seychellois buyers acquire freehold ownership?

Non-Seychellois may acquire privately owned immovable property, subject to Government Sanction and the applicable transaction process. Independent Seychelles legal advice is recommended.

Is residency guaranteed with a purchase?

No. Any residency application is considered separately by the relevant Seychelles authorities and depends on individual circumstances.

Can I rent the villa?

The permitted use, licensing, management and tax treatment depend on whether the property is used for long-term residential letting or short-term tourist accommodation. Confirm the intended model with qualified Seychelles advisers.

What is included in the base price?

The base scope includes the plot, villa structure, turnkey interior finishing, fencing, fitted kitchen, parking and connections to central estate utilities. A pool, premium materials, professional landscaping and furnishing are optional extras. The final scope is set out in the contract.

How many plots are there?

The estate comprises 30 plots. Current status is shown on the masterplan and confirmed by the sales team before reservation.

Can I appoint my own advisers?

Yes. Buyers are welcome to appoint an independent Seychelles lawyer and technical supervisor to review the documents and construction stages.
