FAQ

Before you request details

Can foreign buyers acquire freehold ownership?

Foreign buyers may acquire freehold title to the villa and land, subject to applicable approvals and transaction procedures confirmed with qualified advisers.

Is residency guaranteed with a purchase?

No. Residency support is available, but eligibility is subject to approval by the relevant Seychelles authorities and individual circumstances.

Are rental returns guaranteed?

No. Potential rental scenarios depend on market conditions, occupancy and management. Rental rates and occupancy figures are indicative only and are not guaranteed.

What is included in the displayed price?

The displayed price includes the plot, villa construction, interior finishing, landscaping and estate infrastructure. A private pool and pool terrace are optional upgrades at additional cost. Current specifications and pricing are confirmed individually.

How many plots are there?

The estate comprises 30 plots. Current availability is shown on the masterplan. Adjacent available plots may be combined, subject to technical review and final documentation.

Can the villa design be customised?

Individual adaptations may be discussed with the project team. Their feasibility, timing and cost are confirmed for the selected plot and design.
